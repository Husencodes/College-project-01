About Lord Ganesha

Discover the wisdom, symbolism, and eco-friendly traditions of Ganesh Chaturthi.

Who is Lord Ganesha?

Lord Ganesha is one of the most revered deities in Hinduism, known as the remover of obstacles...

He is the son of Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati. Honored at the start of rites and ceremonies, Ganesha is also worshipped as the patron of sciences, arts, and the deva of intellect and wisdom.

🎉

Ganesh Chaturthi

Ganesh Chaturthi is a grand festival marking the rebirth of Lord Ganesha...

Spanning 10 days, the festival starts with installing clay idols in homes and public pandals, culminating in the festive procession and immersion (Visarjan) in water bodies.

🌱

Eco-Friendly Celebration

Protecting Mother Earth while celebrating traditions is vital for modern festivities...

Using clay idols (Clay Ganesha), natural organic dyes, and artificial immersion tanks prevents water pollution and protects aquatic life, preserving nature for future generations.

☸️

Sacred Symbols

Every detail of Ganesha's form carries deep symbolic philosophical meaning...

The large head signifies wisdom and broad thinking; large ears signify listening more; the trunk denotes adaptability; and the Modak symbolizes the sweet reward of spiritual devotion.
